A steampunk parlour for coffee, scones & a good book.

SteamPunk Cafe & Coffee House is a steampunk-themed café in Port Alberni — in its own words, family owned and operated — with a free community library lounge to settle into while you sip.

3025a 3rd Ave, Port Alberni, BC · Mon–Wed 7:30 AM–4 PM · Thu–Fri 7:30 AM–5 PM · Sat 8 AM–4 PM · Sun closed · (778) 421-3000

✦ ✦ ✦

From the Bakery Case

Lavender blackberry scones and gluten-free muffins — with GF and vegan options on the menu, so there is something in the case for everyone.

From the Coffee Bar

A Dirty Chai Latte, a peppermint mocha, or an espresso milkshake — the drinks lean as playful as the parlour around them.

From the Kitchen

A vegan panini and butter chicken soup headline the savoury side — lunch with the library lounge waiting right beside it.

The Library Lounge

A free community library lounge is part of the café itself: pick a book, find a seat, and stay a while over coffee.
